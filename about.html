Saffron Crumb is a home bakery in Lahore, built around a simple idea: a freshly baked treat can make an ordinary day feel special. We make cakes, cookies, brownies and savoury favourites with familiar ingredients and a little extra care.

Whether it is something sweet for tea or a bake to share, we are happy to help you find a kitchen favourite.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you order.

Which areas of Lahore do you deliver to?

Delivery availability depends on your area. Share your neighbourhood when you enquire, and we’ll confirm whether delivery is available.

How much notice should I give?

Please order as early as you can. We’ll confirm the soonest available pickup date when you get in touch.

Can I pay when I pick up?

Payment is due when you collect your order. Please confirm accepted payment methods when placing your order.

Can I request a custom cake?

Share your preferred size, flavour and design. We’ll confirm what we can make and the price before you decide.

What if I have a food allergy?

Please tell us before ordering so we can discuss ingredients. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free preparation.
